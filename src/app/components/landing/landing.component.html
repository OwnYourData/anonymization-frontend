How it works

The service follows a configuration-driven approach: you define how each attribute should be treated (based on datatype and your data model). The anonymisation pipeline then applies the selected operators consistently.

1

3

FAQ

Is the output truly "anonymous"? ▸
It depends on your data and configuration. The tool applies the transformations you choose, but true anonymity also depends on context, dataset size, and external data availability. Always review output critically and consider using a new issue template to report edge cases.
What formats are supported? ▸
JSON and JSON-LD. The pipeline preserves your document structure while transforming the values you specify. Support for additional formats is on the roadmap.
How do I provide good feedback? ▸
A short, concrete repro — your sample input, the configuration you used, and what you expected vs. what you got — is the most helpful. Open an issue on github.com/OwnYourData/anonymisation-service.
Can I run this in my own environment? ▸
Yes. The project is open source. Clone the repo and follow the setup instructions in the README. You can host the service wherever you need it.
What data should I avoid uploading? ▸
Never upload real personal data during testing. Always use synthetic or non-sensitive samples. Effective anonymisation is context-dependent; treat output as risk-reduced rather than "guaranteed anonymous."
